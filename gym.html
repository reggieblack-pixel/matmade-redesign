About Gracie Barra Austin

Gracie Barra Austin is one of Austin's most established Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu academies, part of the world's largest BJJ organization founded by Master Carlos Gracie Jr. Training here means access to a proven, structured curriculum that has developed thousands of black belts worldwide.

Whether you're stepping onto the mat for the first time or preparing for your next competition, GB Austin offers morning and evening classes seven days a week. The academy maintains a welcoming atmosphere without sacrificing intensity — students at every rank are expected to train smart and train hard.
