Biography

About Prof. Marcus

Professor Marcus Almeida began his BJJ journey in São Paulo, Brazil in 2007 under the Gracie Barra banner. He received his black belt in 2018 from Professor Carlos Gracie Jr. after competing successfully on the IBJJF circuit throughout his purple, brown, and early black belt years.

After relocating to Austin in 2013, Marcus joined the local Gracie Barra affiliate as an assistant instructor and eventually took over as head instructor in 2016. His teaching philosophy centers on precision over speed — he believes that most errors on the mat are mechanical, not strategic, and that a student who understands the structure of a position can solve any problem within it.

Outside of the academy, Marcus competes as a masters athlete at major IBJJF events and regularly travels to train at the GB headquarters in California. He holds seminars twice yearly and mentors a group of brown belt students working toward their black belts.
