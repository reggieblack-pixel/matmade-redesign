Built for the BJJ community.

MatMade is the world's largest Brazilian Jiu Jitsu gym directory — built by practitioners, for practitioners.

Founded 2020 Kennesaw, GA Small team, big mission

MatMade started with a simple question: why is it so hard to find a BJJ gym that's actually a good fit?

When Kenny Kim moved to a new city and tried to find a gym, he spent two weeks searching through outdated Google listings, Facebook pages that hadn't been updated since 2017, and websites with broken links and wrong phone numbers. When he finally found a gym he liked, he had to call three times before someone picked up.

That frustration turned into MatMade — a directory built not for search engines, but for people who actually want to train.

What We're Building

MatMade is the most complete, most accurate, most up-to-date BJJ and martial arts gym directory in the world. We're obsessed with data quality — which means verifying listings, correcting outdated information, and making sure every gym that's listed actually deserves to be found.

  • 8,400+ gym listings across all 50 states
  • 3,000+ cities with dedicated location pages
  • Verified listings for gyms that have been confirmed by the gym owner or the MatMade team
  • 84 community stories collected from practitioners around the country

"The mat doesn't care where you came from. It only cares about what you do next. That's the ethos behind everything we build."

The Show

Beyond the directory, MatMade produces a documentary series called The MatMade Show — short films about how Jiu Jitsu changes people's lives. Stories about overcoming addiction, surviving grief, losing weight, finding confidence, and building community.

Four seasons in, with over 2 million views, The Show has become something we're deeply proud of. Every episode is free. Every story is real.

For Gym Owners

If you run a BJJ or martial arts academy, MatMade is the best place to be found by students actively looking for a gym. Claiming your listing is free. Upgrading to Premium or Pro gives you a full marketing page — hero images, instructor profiles, scheduling links, and priority in search results.

The Team

MatMade is run by a small team of people who train. We don't have investors. We don't have a marketing department. We have a strong belief that the BJJ community deserves better tools.
